# Code of conduct

Vela welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Treat contributors and users with respect. Ask questions in good faith, give specific technical feedback, and make room for different perspectives.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's personal data or private messages without permission. Critique ideas and code rather than attacking people.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces maintained for Vela. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation proportionately. Maintainers are expected to explain decisions when practical and protect reporter privacy.

If the repository provides a private reporting channel, use it for sensitive conduct reports. A dedicated private contact has not yet been configured; do not expose private details in public. For an immediate moderation need, flag the relevant content through the hosting platform and request maintainer attention without republishing the harmful material. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for technical vulnerabilities.
